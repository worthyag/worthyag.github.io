Exploratory Data Analysis.

Trendy or Body Dysmorphia?

In this project, I conducted an in-depth analysis of Generation Z's internet and social media usage patterns and their potential impact on mental health. Through extensive data collection and analysis, I explored various aspects, including the frequency and duration of social media usage, online activities, preference for visual platforms, and the prevalence of mental health issues such as anxiety and depression. My findings suggest a strong correlation between high social media engagement and mental health concerns among Generation Z, highlighting the need for further research and intervention in this area. By uncovering these insights, I aim to contribute to a better understanding of the complex relationship between technology use and mental well-being in today's youth.

I accomplished the following:

  • Conducted comprehensive analysis of Generation Z's internet and social media usage patterns.
  • Explored frequency and duration of social media usage, online activities, and preference for visual platforms.
  • Investigated potential correlations between social media engagement and mental health issues.
  • Identified prevalence of anxiety and depression among Generation Z based on data analysis.
  • Emphasized the need for further research and intervention to address mental health concerns in youth.
  • Demonstrated strong analytical and research skills through extensive data collection and interpretation.
  • Contributed valuable insights to understanding the impact of technology on mental well-being in today's youth.
