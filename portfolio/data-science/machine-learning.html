Machine Learning.

Is your car draining your wallet?

Is your car draining your wallet is an ML exploratory data analysis that I conducted for a university project (we were free to pick any subject area). The purpose of this project was for us to gain imperative data science skills. The aim was to do the following:

  • Build a linear regression model.
  • Write computer programs that can read, process and analyse textual and numerical data.
  • Generate plots and interactive visualisations of data.
  • Apply statistical methods to the interpretation of results.
  • Use data analysis in the decision-making process.

My project title was "Is your car draining your wallet? Exploring the ways in which fuel efficiency (MPG) is impacted". I learnt the skills used daily by data scientists, I learnt machine learning principles and applications. You can view the project to learn more about it and the process I took.
